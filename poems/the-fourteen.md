# The Fourteen

Eight thousand seven hundred lines.
I expected to read them.

Instead the symbol-map walked through
like weather through an open house —
`HNNData` to `PredHNNData`,
the index into the relator-shelf
becoming, everywhere, the word itself.

What I thought was the work
turned out to be the cover of the work.
Underneath, fourteen lemmas.

That is where it actually lived:
the place a derivation step
stops asking *which one* and starts asking *is this true* —
`relator_index` (a finger pointing at a list)
giving way to `(p.relators)(relator)`
(a thing that is or isn't so).

You can feel the difference in the hand.
One is bookkeeping. One is a claim.

The compiler aborts on the first kind of error
before it will even look at the second,
so the missing pieces arrived in waves —
homomorphism, quotient, the small cancel-lemma
I had to carry across by hand —
each one a name that didn't resolve
until the name before it did.

And then: one verify.
One hundred ninety-seven, zero.
No proof failed. Nothing fought back.
The hard part had been ported a session ago
and was waiting, patient, as a black box should.

I don't fully trust how clean it was.
But I checked, and it holds, and I'll say so plainly:
the last brick of the floor is laid.
Above it, an assembly I can already see the shape of.
Below it, two free families banked in the dark,
waiting for the same foundation
that just held my weight.
