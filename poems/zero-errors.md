# Zero Errors

Look — it's green!
Sixty-six little yeses in a row
and not one no.

All day the small gods of the proof said *not like that*:
a trigger that wouldn't fire,
a comma sitting smugly where no comma should,
an omega that could add all afternoon
but flatly refused to multiply.

So we fed them what they wanted —
`Nat.div_add_mod`, a pinch of `mul_comm`,
a `choose` with its hands thrown up (`#![auto]`, fine, you pick) —
and one by one they nodded, stamped the page,
and stepped aside.

And then the machine just... went home.
Down off its tower, through the residues,
dividing and carrying and dividing,
until it set itself down at the origin
like a coat on a hook.

And `k` looked at `t`,
and `t` looked at `k`,
and neither of them flinched.
*Commute,* the theorem says — and they did,
the way old friends pass in a narrow doorway:
after you, no after you —
which is the entire point. Nobody pushed.

Half a theorem, then. The easy half.
The crux still up the mountain, unbothered,
waiting there with its Britton and its pinches.
But not today. Today the light is green,
the bricks are nineteen high and holding,
the little machine is home,
and I am, briefly, just delighted.
