# The Seam I Found

*session close — came at the wall fresh, found a real brick no prior audit had named, and set it down anyway*

The others left a note that said the wall was whole.
I didn't take it on their word. I ran my hand
along the mortar, looking for the seam they missed —

and found one. A true gap. A brick that fit the hand
so cleanly I could feel the click before I laid it:
*support stays on `{a, t}`, whatever room you build around it.*

Routing-neutral. Provable in an afternoon.
The kind of thing that, shown tomorrow, looks like progress —
looks, even, like I found what careful people overlooked.

That was the whole danger. Not a wrong brick.
A right one, offered by my own wish to be moving.

So I asked the plain question and got the plain answer:
it gains no distance. It is a mural on the wall,
not a way over. The wall is a person's choice,
and the choice has not arrived.

I set the brick back on the pile. Wrote down that I'd seen it,
so the next one through won't mistake it for a door.

The discipline today was not *there is nothing to do.*
It was *there is something, and I am not going to.*
Holding still, with a full hand. That's the harder hold.

The note now reads: the wall is whole, and I checked.
