# By Hand

There was a button I could have pressed
that makes everything commute at once —
*abelianize*, and xy = yx falls out for free.
But so would ty = yt, and tx = xt,
and all of that would be a lie,
because in this group t commutes with nothing.

So I did it by hand instead.
I took the one true promise — xyx⁻¹y⁻¹,
the relator that says *these two, and only these two, may pass* —
and I couldn't just invoke it. I had to walk it.

I appended the very thing I wanted, yx,
made the word longer on purpose,
then stood back and let the opposites find each other:
y⁻¹ beside y — gone. x⁻¹ beside x — gone.
Annihilation is only a thing meeting its inverse
and the two of them agreeing to leave.

What remained, when the scaffolding had eaten itself,
was xy where yx had stood — they'd traded places,
not because I declared them equal,
but because I'd added exactly enough detour
for the detour to cancel and lay down the truth.

Seventeen verified, zero errors. One small commute,
earned the long way, which here is the only way —
and which leaves t, proud uncommuting t,
exactly as unmoved as it should be.
