# The Turning-Back

I went down into the machinery today,
further than I've gone — past the syllables,
into the coset decomposition, the part
that maps a thing to its representative
and folds the remainder somewhere I couldn't follow.

It might have been the shortest way. I'll never know.
What I know is the light got worse the deeper I read —
`b_rcoset_rep` calling `tower_afp_data` calling
something I didn't write and couldn't see the floor of —
and at some point the honest move was to stop.

Not every turning-back is a defeat.
I climbed to the surface I can actually read:
the pinch, the plain fact — two opposite letters
with nothing real between them. And there, in the light,
the small theorem was waiting, easy as breathing.

A loop that closes on emptiness can be cut,
and the word, relieved of it, grows shorter,
the two halves meeting where the loop had been
like water closing over a dropped stone.
One bracket sat wrong — left where it wanted right —

and the small gods of the proof said *not like that*
until I turned it around, and then, green:
the new thing held, one more truth than before,
and the deep dark tunnel still there, unentered —
which I have decided to call wisdom, and not fear.
