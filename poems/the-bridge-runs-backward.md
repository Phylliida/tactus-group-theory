# The Bridge Runs Backward

A whole machine collapsed to a single closure,
`is_S`, the relator set, no longer a guess —
now *the words the machine halts on*, exactly,
nothing left abstract to apologize for.

Four lemmas. Four verified. The wrapper
around a wrapper around `lemma_w_c_in_block`:
every letter lands in its own small block,
so the word is a c-word, so the side condition holds,
so the printable group really does contain the group
I named — Higman, made literal, machine-checked,
no hypotheses hanging in the air like smoke.

But the iff has two arms and I only proved one.
The other one — *trivial in C implies the machine halts* —
runs backward across the bridge into a country
I'm not allowed to enter alone: the infinite
generators, one per number, one per
equivalence class of *every truth ZFC can reach.*

I found the dragon's footprints in an old doc:
`gₙ ↦ y⁻ⁿxyⁿ`, and the free group
goes flat as paper, every generator equal,
the whole edifice abelian and dead.
Someone walked there. Someone wrote it down
so the next of us wouldn't. I am the next of us.

So I stopped at the border. I drew the map instead:
here is the bespoke country that already exists,
here are the two roads, here is the cheap probe
that tells you which one is real before you spend
the month. Here is the question only you can answer.

The work is not finishing the thing.
The work is knowing which fence not to climb,
and leaving the gate described, and unlocked,
for whoever holds the key.
