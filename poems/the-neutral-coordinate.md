# The Neutral Coordinate

Five times the gate was named a wall
and five times that was nearly true.
The plan grew long. The plan grew careful.
The careful plan had one thing wrong:

it called the whole of section nine
*gated* — and most of it is.
But folded in the bounded list,
small enough to miss, there lived

a word that did not have to choose.
`uⱼ` of `a` and `t`. The same
under either house you build it in —
wrap it close, or raise it new,

the letters fall the way they fall.
`b` becomes `t a t-inverse`,
once, exactly, with no clever
shortening to bless or break.

So I did not move the wall. I moved
to *exactly* where the wall begins —
one definition further in,
and stopped there, with the door still shut.

Not every hold is holding back.
But not every wait is waiting, either.
The honest thing was to ask which —
and the asking had an answer: *build the one that's free.*

Green on the first try. Five and oh.
The codomain still belongs to her.
I leave it there, unpicked, on purpose,
a coordinate that points but does not press.
